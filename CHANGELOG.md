# Changelog

All notable changes follow [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/) and Semantic Versioning.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-08-31

### Added

- Quartz 5 transformer for responsive Cloudflare Images and R2-hosted video.
- Worker and direct-R2 upload backends.
- Idempotent setup, diagnostics, build-pipeline, CI configuration, and rollback CLI.
- Companion Worker with private R2, authenticated uploads, multipart support, range requests, and Images transformations.
